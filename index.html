ChabadOne Ads Assistant

ChabadOne Ads Assistant is an internal tool used by Mendel Gordon of the ChabadOne team at Chabad.org to manage the Google Ads accounts in the ChabadOne Sites manager account. These are the advertising accounts, many of them Google Ad Grants accounts, that ChabadOne runs for Chabad centers.

The tool reads campaign, ad and performance data through the Google Ads API, prepares changes such as pausing ads, fixing disapproved ads, and updating keywords, bids and budgets, and applies a change only after Mendel reviews and approves it.

It is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it.
